# Measure implementation, discovery and business outcomes separately

[English](MEASUREMENT.md) | [简体中文](MEASUREMENT.zh-CN.md) | [日本語](MEASUREMENT.ja.md) | [繁體中文](MEASUREMENT.zh-HK.md)

A local export with correct canonicals can pass its checks while a live page still has a delivery problem. A cited AI answer can mention a company without sending a visitor. A form click can happen without an inquiry. Record each outcome using its own evidence.

## Use a measurement plan

| Layer | Evidence | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementation | Export revision, checks, browser task result | Live deployment or indexing |
| Delivery | Real status, final URL, served language, deployed asset | Search visibility |
| Search discovery | Page/query/region/device and complete date window | A qualified business opportunity |
| Answer visibility | Exact prompt, platform, date, cited URL and quotation | Clicks or account-wide coverage |
| Business | Confirmed receipt, relevant request and business follow-up | Cause from a single content change |

Here SEO means search discovery and website fundamentals. AEO and GEO are working labels for answer visibility and generative-answer visibility; they do not create a separate validated ranking metric. Inspect an answer's actual citation and whether it represents the company correctly.

## Compare like with like

Choose pages and the outcome before changing content. Record deployment and recrawl times. Keep region, device, queries and date-window length consistent. Annotate seasonal demand, campaigns and other releases. With small samples, report raw counts and uncertainty rather than turning one new inquiry into a percentage-growth headline.

An example plan is to review a fixed set of service pages over four to eight weeks, but that is an observation window, not a guarantee of a result. A local heuristic pass, file-size reduction and AI mention do not prove rank or conversion uplift. Use the [tracking worksheet](https://github.com/awesomellm/website-migration-kit/blob/main/tracking-plan.csv) to define fields, owners and acceptance evidence without collecting visitors' form text.

## Decide the next change from evidence

Fix a demonstrated delivery or content mismatch first. Improve a page that answers a real buyer decision and can carry original evidence. Merge related pages only after sustained query/page evidence and a relevant replacement plan; use the [migration process](https://github.com/awesomellm/website-migration-kit/blob/main/MIGRATION.md). Google's [content evaluation guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content?hl=en) supports original, useful material with clear authorship and evidence. Apply that review to GitHub documentation as well as website pages.
