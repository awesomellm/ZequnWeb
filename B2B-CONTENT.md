# B2B content starts with a buyer decision

[English](B2B-CONTENT.md) | [简体中文](B2B-CONTENT.zh-CN.md) | [日本語](B2B-CONTENT.ja.md) | [繁體中文](B2B-CONTENT.zh-HK.md)

A useful product page lets a buyer determine the model, specification, quantity constraints, destination and next action. A fictional DEMO-02 page may show how that works, but must stay labelled as a demonstration. Real product claims require approved data.

| Topic | Content candidates | Publishable decision and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope and delivery | Website brief, one-page versus multipage, AI builder versus custom work, timeline and handover | Define audience, required pages, functions, owners and acceptance before quoting. |
| Products and variants | Product data, model pages, comparisons, OEM/ODM and public price versus quote | Use stable model ids, real differences, units and explicit quote dependencies. |
| Export inquiries | MOQ, samples, lead time, WhatsApp, quote planning and traffic without leads | Answer purchasing conditions; retain model and destination; distinguish draft, receipt and qualified demand. |
| Trust and production | About, manufacturing evidence, factory video, certificates and test reports | Identify what is actually shown, its date, issuer and scope; seek permission; keep concept work labelled. |
| Files and expertise | PDF catalogues, CAD libraries, technical glossaries and open versus gated downloads | Provide versioned files, accessible page context and correct model/language mapping; explain access conditions. |
| Page roles | Service versus article intent, application pages, genuine location pages and dealer directories | Assign one buyer decision per page; substantiate locations and partners instead of cloning place names. |
| Lifecycle and indexing | Discontinued products, filters, canonical/noindex/robots decisions, migration and no-result searches | Choose a relevant replacement or truthful retirement; inspect real responses and avoid accidental thin index targets. |
| AI and discovery | AI company corrections, technical AI review, visibility audit, SEO/AEO/GEO priorities | Keep a public source of verified facts and review generated claims; separately record citation, visit and receipt. |
| Operations and learning | Maintenance budget, SEO deliverables, case evidence, measurement and trade-show lifecycle | Define evidence, update owners and dates; maintain pre-event, event and follow-up content without invented results. |
| Language delivery | Multilingual products and equivalent pages | Maintain one product identity, approved translations, language-specific files and responsibility for updates. |

Before publishing, name the reader question, page role, source, units, owner, review date and next action. Separate facts from estimates; remove a claim that cannot be supported. Review AI-assisted technical text against source drawings, tests or approved specifications, not against another generated answer.

Use the [product worksheet](https://github.com/awesomellm/website-migration-kit/blob/main/product-data.csv), [content collection worksheet](https://github.com/awesomellm/website-migration-kit/blob/main/content-collection.csv) and [case evidence worksheet](https://github.com/awesomellm/website-migration-kit/blob/main/case-evidence.csv). Start with a few complete pages and reusable records. Publish further pages when they answer a distinct question and contain real supporting material. Google’s [content self-evaluation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content?hl=en) asks creators to assess original value and evidence; a keyword alone is not a content brief.
